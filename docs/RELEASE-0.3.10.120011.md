# Building Pieces 0.3.10 for Minecraft 1.20.1

This update improves snow, grass and crafting on Forge 47.4.10. Existing
pieces keep their block IDs, materials and orientations.

Snow pieces can be picked back up after breaking them by hand, and a shovel
still breaks them faster. A snow piece or snowy grass piece resting on a
grass block gives the exposed grass a snowy appearance too. Taking the piece
away restores the normal texture without changing the saved grass block.

Grass pieces spread throughout the world's build height and turn back into
dirt under solid cover or water. When Grass Slabs is installed, both mods use
its shared grass rules.

Recipe-book buttons keep different results separate. Duplicate step-rebuilding
recipes have been removed, and dirt vertical slabs can still be rebuilt into
dirt when Building Pieces is used on its own. Wooden pieces use the matching
full material's fuel value, scaled to the size of the piece.

## Trying the update

Back up an existing world before updating. Check horizontal and vertical slab
placement, joins, grass and path pieces, snowy steps and corners, glass walls,
and the recipe book. If Mineralogy or Biomes O' Plenty is installed, check
pieces made from those materials too. Normal placement stays available through
the Directional slab placement setting.
