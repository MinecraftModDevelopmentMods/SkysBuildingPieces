# Sky's Building Pieces 0.3.8.121011

This version is for Minecraft 1.21.1 with Forge 52.1.0 and Java 21.

The same building pieces and recipes are available, including continuous glass
walls and the grass steps and corners. Pieces keep their saved identities and
orientation when you upgrade a world.

Grass Slabs is optional. When you install it, grass can spread between slabs,
steps and corners. Mineralogy is also optional and adds matching steps and
corners for its natural rocks.

Back up your world before changing Minecraft versions, and try the upgrade on a
copy first.
