# Building Pieces for Minecraft 1.20.1

Building Pieces is available for Forge 47.4.10 and Java 17. Your pieces still
use the same material slots and recipes, and the creative tab keeps them
together with the vanilla shapes they reuse.

Glass walls keep their continuous joins and their borders at posts, exposed
ends and colour changes. Grass Slabs and Mineralogy remain optional. Building
Pieces does not generate terrain.

Back up your world before upgrading and try a copy first. See the
[world upgrade guide](WORLD-UPGRADES.md) for the older pieces this mod can
recover.
