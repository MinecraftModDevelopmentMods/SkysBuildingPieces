# Sky's Building Pieces 0.3.10.2602001

Snow pieces can be picked up by hand, and a shovel still digs them faster.
Snow-covered grass steps and corners follow nearby snowy terraces and cold
surfaces. Grass below snowy pieces gets a matching snow-covered top and sides.

Wooden pieces burn for their share of a full block's fuel time. Cutting wood
into smaller pieces no longer increases the amount of fuel it provides.

The recipe book groups recipes by their result and keeps distinct cuts visible.
Duplicate step-combining recipes are removed, and dirt vertical slabs can still
be combined when Grass Slabs isn't installed. The renderer also handles missing
model data safely when other mods request a piece's appearance.

Existing block IDs, orientations, configuration choices and migration progress
are preserved.
