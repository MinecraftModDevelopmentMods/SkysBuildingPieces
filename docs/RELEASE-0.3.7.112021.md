# Sky's Building Pieces 0.3.7 for Minecraft 1.12.2

Dirt and grass steps and corners can now be flattened with a shovel. A grass
block beneath a grass piece becomes a path in the same action, using one point
of tool durability. The pieces keep their shape and orientation.

Grass and snowy edges now follow the smaller pieces properly, and exposed
ground beneath steps and corners can stay grassy. When Grass Slabs is
installed, use version 1.1.1.112021 or later for the shared grass behaviour.

Two matching corners stacked in the crafting grid make a step; two steps make
a slab. This also works with the BOP add-on's materials. Existing native
recipes keep their normal results.

Mineralogy steps and corners are included automatically when Mineralogy is
installed. No separate rock-piece add-on is needed. Pieces saved by the earlier
test add-on keep their materials, orientations and item data.

Building Pieces still works on its own and does not generate terrain.
